LampOff Studio · Beta

Stage lighting, before the stage.

LampOff Studio is a previsualisation program that opens a lighting plot made in LampOff in a realistic 3D stage built on Unreal Engine. With haze, gobos, shadows and real fixture models, the design is visible before the first rehearsal.

Windows · macOS · coming soon

In beta · English and Turkish

Light and haze

Real-time light and haze

Haze shows only where light passes; beams do not hang in the air but end on the surface they hit. Global illumination, reflections and shadows build the stage like a real auditorium.

  • Volumetric haze with adjustable density
  • Performers, scenery, truss and fixture bodies cast shadows
  • House light: dark, show and work

Fixtures

Real fixture models

Fixtures arrive with the 3D geometry from their GDTF file; base, yoke and head are separate parts and pan/tilt turn on the real axes. The lens glows in the colour of the light.

  • GDTF geometry and beam angle
  • Fallback model per type for fixtures without one
  • Truss, pipes, towers and FOH at real dimensions

Gobo and prism

Sharp, steady gobos

The gobo pattern sits in the same frame on the floor, on the drape and in the beam in haze; it does not flicker or smear at steep angles. Prism and frost work on the same fixture.

  • Eight built-in gobo patterns
  • Three-facet prism
  • Soft edge with frost

Project

Opens LampOff projects directly

A LampOff project prepared on a phone or tablet opens in the 3D stage with the same pipes, fixtures, focus points and colours. No file conversion between plot and previsualisation.

  • Pipe heights and tower fixtures
  • Focus points and gel colours
  • Drapes, wings, platforms, scenery and performer figures

Camera

From the house, the stage, above

Preset views switch between house, downstage, top and both sides with one key; a free camera reaches every corner of the stage. Each fixture can be viewed up close in turn.

  • Five preset views and free camera
  • Close-up from fixture to fixture
  • Exposure control and high-resolution screenshots
