Light and haze

Haze only where the light passes.

Beams do not hang in the air; they end on the surface they hit. Global illumination, reflections and shadows build the stage like a real auditorium.

Volumetric hazeAdjustable density.
Real shadowsPerformers, scenery, truss and fixture bodies cast shadows.
House lightDark, show and work modes.

Fixtures

Real fixture models.

Fixtures arrive with the 3D geometry from their GDTF file; base, yoke and head are separate parts and pan/tilt turn on the real axes. The lens glows in the colour of the light.

GDTF geometryBody parts and beam angle come from the file.
Fallback modelsA fallback model per type for fixtures without one.
Real dimensionsTruss, pipes, towers and FOH placed at real size.

Gobo and prism

Sharp, steady gobos

The gobo pattern sits in the same frame on the floor, on the drape and in the beam in haze; it does not flicker or smear at steep angles.

  • Eight built-in gobo patterns
  • Three-facet prism
  • Soft edge with frost

Project

The whole stage comes along

Not only the fixtures: pipe heights, drapes, wings and scenery open in their places from the plot.

  • Pipe heights and tower fixtures
  • Focus points and gel colours
  • Drapes, wings, platforms, scenery and performer figures

Camera

From the house, the stage, above

Preset views switch between house, downstage, top and both sides with one key; a free camera reaches every corner of the stage.

  • Five preset views and free camera
  • Close-up from fixture to fixture
  • Exposure control and high-resolution screenshots
