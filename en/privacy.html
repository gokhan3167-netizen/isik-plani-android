Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

LampOff is an app for preparing stage lighting plots and technical riders. This page explains how the app handles personal data.

Data collected

The app collects no personal data, has no accounts and does not track you. It contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools.

Storage

Your plots, fixture library and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to third parties.

Files and sharing

When you export a PDF, PNG, CSV or project file, it is created on your device and the system share sheet opens. Only you decide where it goes (for example WhatsApp or e-mail). Imported GDTF, OFL or QLC+ files are processed only on your device.

Internet use

The app works offline. It connects only when you ask it to (for example to download the current fixture list from the online library) or to check for a new version. No personal data is sent.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no visitor tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.
